Abstract:
E-commerce platforms have become an important part of modern shopping, where customers
expect personalized product recommendations and fast service. Most existing recommendation
systems use purchase history, browsing history, ratings, and reviews to suggest products.
Although these systems improve the shopping experience, they still face several challenges,
including limited personalization, dependence on historical data, poor real-time adaptation, lack
of recommendation transparency, and reduced performance during periods of high server traffic.
These limitations affect both customer satisfaction and overall system efficiency.
To address these challenges, this project proposes an Adaptive E-Commerce Recommendation
System Using Big Data Analytics, Digital Twin Simulation, and AI-Based Traffic
Optimization. The system analyzes customer engagement factors such as product views, clicks,
searches, cart activities, purchases, ratings, and reviews to better understand customer
preferences. Big Data Analytics is used to process large volumes of customer data efficiently,
while a Multi-Agent AI framework generates personalized recommendations based on user
behavior.
The proposed system also integrates Digital Twin Simulation to virtually evaluate
recommendation strategies before they are presented to users, improving recommendation
quality and reliability. Explainable AI (XAI) provides users with clear reasons for each
recommendation, increasing transparency and trust. A Self-Learning Feedback Engine
continuously updates the recommendation model based on user interactions, while the AI-Based
Traffic Optimization module monitors server load and efficiently manages recommendation
requests during peak shopping periods to ensure smooth system performance.
The proposed framework improves recommendation accuracy, scalability, transparency,
response time, and overall customer experience while maintaining efficient resource utilization.
Future improvements may include integrating voice-based shopping assistants, Augmented
Reality (AR) product visualization, blockchain-based review verification, and federated
learning to further enhance personalization, security, privacy, and the overall shopping
experience.
