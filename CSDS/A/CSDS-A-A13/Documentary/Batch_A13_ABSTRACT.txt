Project Title:
AI-Enabled Digital Examination Seating Management System
Abstract:

Examination seating arrangement is a critical administrative task performed by educational institutions before every examination cycle. Traditionally, this process is carried out manually or through semi-automated spreadsheet-based methods, which are time-consuming, error-prone, and often fail to prevent malpractice due to predictable or poorly randomized seating patterns. Manual allocation also struggles to efficiently handle constraints such as room capacity, subject clashes, department-wise distribution, and roll-number sequencing, especially when the number of students, rooms, and examinations increases. The proposed system, an AI-Enabled Digital Examination Seating Management System, addresses these challenges by automating the entire seating allocation process using intelligent algorithms.
The system leverages artificial intelligence and optimization techniques to generate fair, randomized, and constraint-satisfying seating plans within seconds. It considers multiple parameters such as classroom capacity, student count, subject combinations, and anti-cheating spacing rules to ensure that students from the same course or with adjacent roll numbers are not seated close to one another. The platform provides a centralized digital dashboard for administrators to upload student data, configure examination halls, and generate seating charts automatically, which can then be exported, printed, or displayed digitally. Additionally, features such as attendance tracking, real-time seat lookup for students, and automated report generation are integrated to enhance the overall examination management workflow.
By replacing manual effort with an intelligent, automated approach, the proposed system significantly reduces administrative workload, minimizes human error, and strengthens examination integrity through unbiased and unpredictable seating allocation. The solution is designed to be scalable and adaptable for institutions of varying sizes, making it a practical and efficient tool for modern examination management. Overall, this project demonstrates how artificial intelligence can be effectively applied to streamline routine academic administrative processes and improve institutional efficiency.
