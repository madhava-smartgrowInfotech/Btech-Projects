Abstract: 
 
VisionForge AI is an intelligent software-based industrial inspection system designed to improve 
product quality by automating the defect detection process. The system uses deep learning-based 
computer vision techniques to identify defects in industrial products such as Printed Circuit 
Boards (PCBs), steel surfaces, and other manufactured components. Unlike traditional AI models 
that only classify defects, VisionForge AI also explains why a defect was detected by 
highlighting the important regions of the image using Explainable AI techniques. 
The system further enhances the inspection process by generating a simple human-readable 
explanation and suggesting possible root causes for the detected defect. A web-based dashboard 
allows users to upload images, view inspection results, analyze explanation heatmaps, and 
automatically generate professional inspection reports. The reports include defect type, 
confidence score, explanation, possible causes, and recommended corrective actions. 
The proposed system is completely software-based, making it cost-effective and easy to 
implement using publicly available industrial datasets. VisionForge AI aims to improve 
inspection accuracy, reduce manual effort, increase transparency in AI predictions, and support 
faster decision-making in manufacturing industries.
