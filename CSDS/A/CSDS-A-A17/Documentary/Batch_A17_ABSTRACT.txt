Abstract 
 
Agriculture is facing significant challenges due to fluctuating market prices, inconsistent crop 
quality assessment, supply chain inefficiencies, and limited access to real-time decision 
support for farmers. To address these issues, this project, CropSight: Smart Crop Delivery 
with AI-Based Price and Quality Intelligence, presents an intelligent agricultural platform 
that integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Internet of Things (IoT) 
technologies. The system enables farmers to assess crop quality, predict market prices, and 
streamline crop delivery processes through a data-driven and automated approach, thereby 
enhancing profitability and reducing post-harvest losses. 
The proposed framework utilizes image-based crop quality analysis to evaluate factors such 
as size, color, texture, and defects using deep learning models. Simultaneously, machine 
learning algorithms analyze historical market trends, weather conditions, demand patterns, 
and regional price variations to generate accurate crop price predictions. IoT sensors and 
cloud-based services provide real-time monitoring of storage and transportation conditions, 
ensuring that crops maintain their quality throughout the supply chain. The platform also 
incorporates explainable AI techniques to provide transparent insights into quality grading 
and pricing decisions. 
Furthermore, CropSight features a smart delivery recommendation module that connects 
farmers, distributors, and buyers through an integrated digital marketplace. The system 
recommends optimal delivery routes, suitable buyers, and the best time for selling crops to 
maximize returns. By combining quality intelligence, price forecasting, and smart logistics, 
the proposed solution improves supply chain efficiency, promotes fair pricing, reduces 
wastage, and empowers farmers with actionable insights for sustainable and profitable 
agriculture.
