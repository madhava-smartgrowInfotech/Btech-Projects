AquaVision: AI-Powered Smart Water Distribution and Quality Intelligence Platform

Abstract:

Access to safe drinking water and efficient water distribution remain major global challenges due to rapid urbanization, population growth, aging pipeline infrastructure, and climate change. Existing machine learning approaches mainly focus on water quality prediction while overlooking important operational aspects such as intelligent water distribution, demand forecasting, leakage detection, anomaly detection, and decision support, limiting their real-world applicability. To address these challenges, this paper proposes AquaVision: AI-Powered Smart Water Distribution and Quality Intelligence Platform, an integrated software-driven solution that combines Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), predictive analytics, and interactive visualization for comprehensive water resource management. The platform utilizes publicly available water quality datasets, historical distribution records, weather information, and water consumption patterns to build predictive models without relying heavily on expensive IoT infrastructure, making it scalable and cost-effective for both urban and rural environments. An ensemble learning framework consisting of Random Forest, XGBoost, Gradient Boosting, and Decision Tree algorithms is employed to accurately predict water quality and future water demand, while additional models identify distribution imbalances, detect potential pipeline leakages, and recognize abnormal water consumption patterns. Data preprocessing and feature engineering techniques improve prediction accuracy by handling missing values, noisy data, and seasonal variations.

Keywords: AquaVision, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Water Quality Prediction, Smart Water Distribution, Demand Forecasting, Leakage Detection, Anomaly Detection, Predictive Analytics, Ensemble Learning, Random Forest, XGBoost, Gradient Boosting, Decision Tree, Interactive Dashboard, Water Resource Management, Sustainable Water Management, Smart City, Data Visualization, Decision Support System.
