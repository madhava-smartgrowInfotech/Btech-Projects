Title of the project:
AUTOMATIC DETECTION OF DIABETIC RETINOPATHY USING HAAR WAVELET TRANSFORM AND LENET CNN
Abstract:
              Diabetic Retinopathy is one of the leading causes of vision impairment among diabetic patients, mainly because the disease progresses silently during its early stages. Early detection and regular screening are essential to prevent severe vision loss; however, manual diagnosis is often time-consuming and requires experienced ophthalmologists. With the advancement of deep learning and medical image analysis, automated retinal disease detection systems have become an effective support tool for clinical screening.
                This project proposes a deep learning–based system for detecting hypertensive retinopathy from retinal fundus images using a hybrid approach that combines the Haar Wavelet Transform for enhanced feature extraction and the LeNet Convolutional Neural Network for accurate classification. The retinal images are preprocessed and resized to a uniform dimension before being used for model training and evaluation. In addition to retinal analysis, the system also evaluates heart disease risk using clinical parameters such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol level, and other relevant health factors. Since retinal blood vessels reflect systemic vascular conditions, changes in the retina can be associated with cardiovascular health.
                 Therefore, integrating heart disease risk factors along with retinal image analysis provides a more comprehensive health assessment. The developed web-based system allows users to upload retinal images and provide heart disease risk data through a user-friendly interface. The final output of the system provides three results: hypertensive retinopathy prediction from the retinal image, heart disease risk prediction based on clinical parameters, and a combined risk stage that integrates both analyses to indicate the overall health risk level. This integrated approach supports early diagnosis, reduces manual screening effort, assists healthcare professionals in making timely medical decisions, and has the potential for real-time integration into healthcare systems, contributing to early intervention and the prevention of vision loss.
Keywords: Diabetic Retinopathy, Haar Wavelet Transform, LeNet CNN, Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), Deep Learning, Retinal, Fundus Images, Medical Image Analysis, Feature Extraction, Image Preprocessing, Image Classification, Heart Disease Risk Prediction, Early Detection
