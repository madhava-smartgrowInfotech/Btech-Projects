Abstract: 
The rapid growth of the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has made digital payments faster, 
simpler, and more convenient. However, it has also increased frauds such as phishing attacks, 
fake payment requests, QR code scams, and social engineering. Existing AI-based fraud 
detection systems can identify suspicious transactions but cannot always prevent users from 
unknowingly sending money to fraudsters. To address this problem, we propose UPI Guardian, 
an AI-Based Adaptive Fraud Prevention Framework that performs AI Risk Analysis using 
transaction behavior, payment history, receiver trust score, and suspicious SMS indicators. Based 
on the calculated risk, the system activates Smart Payment Intent Verification, Delayed 
Protection Mode, and AI Explainable Risk Analysis for high-risk transactions. The framework 
also provides Personalized Multilingual Voice Assistance in English, Hindi, and Telugu to 
improve accessibility. Overall, UPI Guardian aims to make digital payments safer, smarter, more 
secure, and user-friendly. 
 
Keywords: UPI, Fraud Prevention, AI Risk Analysis, Trust Score, Smart Payment Intent 
Verification, Delayed Protection Mode, Explainable AI, Multilingual Voice Assistance.
