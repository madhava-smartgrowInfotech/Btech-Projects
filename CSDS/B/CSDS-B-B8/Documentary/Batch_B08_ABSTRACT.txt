Abstract: 
 
Human Activity Recognition (HAR) is an important application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) 
that enables the automatic identification of human activities for applications such as healthcare, 
elderly care, smart homes, surveillance, and human-computer interaction. Existing HAR systems 
mainly focus on recognizing daily activities but are limited by small datasets, controlled 
environments, and the absence of fall detection and emergency response mechanisms, reducing 
their effectiveness in real-world scenarios. The proposed Enhanced AI-Based Human Activity 
Recognition with Fall Detection and Emergency Alert System uses vision-based data to 
accurately recognize activities such as walking, standing, sitting, drinking water, and object 
picking. The system employs Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning techniques with a larger 
and more diverse dataset to improve accuracy, robustness, and generalization under different 
environmental conditions, including low-light and partial occlusion scenarios. In addition, an AI-
based Fall Detection module detects accidental falls in real time and automatically generates 
emergency alerts through SMS, email, mobile notifications, or alarms, enabling immediate 
assistance from caregivers or family members. The proposed system provides reliable real-time 
healthcare monitoring, enhances user safety, improves activity recognition performance, and 
offers a practical, scalable, and intelligent solution for elderly care, smart homes, and other real-
world monitoring applications. 
Keywords: Human Activity Recognition (HAR), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Deep Learning, 
Vision-Based Activity Recognition, Fall Detection, Emergency Alert System, Healthcare 
Monitoring, Elderly Care, Smart Homes.
