## 2. What the product is (read from the code)

- `smart_traffic/sim.py` (415 lines): pure-Python 1 s-tick traffic-network simulator. Grid of 2x2 or 3x3 junctions, 4 approaches (N,E,S,W), two-phase signal (phase 0 NS, phase 1 EW), saturation discharge 0.5 veh/s/lane, start-up lost time 2 s, clearance (yellow + all-red) 3 s, link time 12 s between junctions, approach capacity 30 vehicles (spill-back), Poisson arrivals at entry roads (base rates N,S 0.15, E,W 0.10 veh/s) multiplied by demand slider x time-of-day profile (morning peak 08:30, evening peak 17:30). Vehicle mix car 68 %, bike 13 %, bus 6 %, truck 13 %; PCU weights car 1, bike 0.5, bus 3, truck 2, ambulance 1. 5 % of cars are red-light runners; wrong-way driver probability 0.0003 per entry-second.
- Four controllers on identical random demand: `fixed` (30 s split), `adaptive` (queue-actuated, min green 15, max 60, switch if other pressure > 2x current + 4), `emergency` (adaptive + pre-emption at ONE junction using ground-truth light-bar range, 5 s look-ahead; stands in for the base paper), `advanced` (this project: PCU-weighted pressure + 0.7 x ML forecast excess + inbound platoon within 8 s + fairness (head wait / 12) + spill-back damping x0.3 when downstream queue >= CAP-4; emergency look-ahead 15 s = green wave; min green 13).
- `smart_traffic/predictor.py` (59 lines): GradientBoostingRegressor (160 trees, depth 4, lr 0.08, subsample 0.9) forecasting each approach's queue 30 s ahead from 11 features (queue, PCU queue, queue lag 10 s and 30 s, 30 s inflow, inbound 15 s, is_green, green time, is_entry, sin/cos time of day); random 75/25 split; Random Forest and persistence baselines. Trained on 6 simulated runs (alternating 2x2/3x3, demand 0.7-1.6, 1800 s, snapshots every 10 s).
- `smart_traffic/vision.py` (201 lines): YOLOv8n (COCO weights, not fine-tuned) filtered to car/motorcycle/bus/truck; HSV red+blue light-bar cluster detector (red H 0-8/172-180, blue H 100-130, S>=120, V>=190, dilated intersection) used to relabel a vehicle box as `ambulance`; contour-based classical detector for the synthetic overhead scenes; density_report: PCU / capacity 14 -> LOW <0.35, MEDIUM <0.7, HIGH <1.0, JAMMED; recommended green 12 s if emergency else clamp(12+3.2 x PCU, 15, 60).
- Violations (red-light jumping, wrong-way driving) are generated by the simulator from ground-truth events and logged with a plate string and a randomly drawn confidence 0.86-0.99 - there is NO trained violation detector and no tracker. Emergency vehicles are known to the simulated controllers by ground truth; the vision module is not wired into the simulator's control loop.
- `backend/` (FastAPI, ~700 lines in 4 modules): HS256 token auth (stdlib), demo operator account (operator@flowsense.app / Flow@1234), predictor trains in a background thread on start (~6 s), 20 endpoints (below). No database: state is in-memory (one shared live simulation, benchmark cache, last-benchmark violations). `frontend/` React 19 + Vite 8 + Tailwind 4 + Recharts, 8 pages (Landing, Login, Live, Benchmark, Vision, Prediction, Violations, About). Legacy Streamlit app `app.py` (546 lines) remains.
- Ports: backend 8310, frontend 5310. Launch: `run.bat`.

## 3. Datasets (all synthetic or public; nothing private)

- **Simulator-generated data** (no external dataset for control): demand is stochastic (Poisson), seeded; SEEDS 40-44 for benchmarks. The predictor's training set is 6 simulated 1800 s runs under the `adaptive` controller (alternating 2x2 / 3x3; demand 0.7/1.0/1.3/1.6; start hours 7/8/12/17) -> **26988 samples x 11 features**, target = queue 30 s ahead (mean 5.81, std 12.02, max 138).
- **Real traffic frames**: visaitech/vehicle-mixed-traffic-detection (Hugging Face, CC BY 4.0): 293 dashcam frames (640x480), 1,506 boxes, classes pedestrian / 2-wheeler / 3-wheeler / 4-wheeler, South Asian mixed traffic, split by source video (239 train / 54 val - all 293 used here because no model is trained on them).
- **Real ambulance photos**: shravya11/ambulance-images (Hugging Face, CC BY 4.0): 2,121 ambulance and 2,628 non-ambulance photos; the first 300 files (by name) of each folder were used (600 images).
- Bundled Ultralytics sample photo `bus.jpg` (1080x810) for the Vision page demo.
- Base-paper data (not used): 3,130 annotated light-bar images from three Roboflow Universe sets, 2,599/266/265 split; SUMO model of the Souissi roundabout, Rabat.

## 13. Honest gaps (state plainly in every document)

- No SUMO/CARLA: validation uses an in-house simulator whose assumptions (demand, saturation flow, link time) are the author's; results are not comparable in absolute terms with the base paper's SUMO roundabout (ambulance travel 56 s, total waiting 19,887 s).
- Emergency vehicles are identified by ground truth inside the simulator; the vision light-bar detector is a separate demo module and is not closed-loop with the controllers.
- No light-bar/emergency-vehicle detector was trained; the HSV rule fails on real ambulance photos (recall 1.3 %). The base paper's YOLO26m (3,130 images, mAP50 0.73) was not reproduced.
- Vehicle detection is stock YOLOv8n; on real South Asian dashcam traffic its recall is 24 % (all vehicle boxes), 8 % for 2-wheelers, 0 % for 3-wheelers, 59 % for 4-wheelers at conf 0.3. It was not fine-tuned.
- Violation detection (red-light, wrong-way) is simulated from ground-truth events with random confidence values; there is no trained detector, no tracking, no ANPR.
- Inter-junction communication is simulated look-ahead on the transit queue (platoon within 8 s, emergency 15 s), not a network protocol.
- The queue forecaster generalises poorly to congestion levels outside its training runs (leave-one-simulation-out R2 ~0.04 mean); its closed-loop value is ~3 s of mean delay in the ablation.
- The `advanced` controller is only ~4-5 % better than plain queue-actuated control (within one std) and is worse than adaptive at very low demand; most of the headline -35 % vs fixed-time comes from adaptivity itself.
- No database, no persistence, one shared live simulation (single demo operator). Auth is a demo account.
- No real-camera video pipeline, no hardware-in-the-loop, no deployment trial.
