How VenturePulse works

  1. Enter structured signals (funding, rounds, milestones, relationships, industry, investors) plus a business description.
  2. VenturePulse builds a fused representation: scaled structured features (weight 0.5) + transformer text embedding, compared with cosine similarity.
  3. It retrieves comparable historical startups and predicts with the best validated classifier.
  4. You see the estimated outcome, confidence, evidence cases, and key contributing features.
