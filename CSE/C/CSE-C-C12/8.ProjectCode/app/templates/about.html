About CareLens AI

CareLens AI is a local symptom-monitoring and preliminary health-guidance assistant. It retrieves relevant entries from a local medical knowledge base and presents them with clear safety notices.

What it does

  • Accepts symptom descriptions in everyday language
  • Finds the most relevant knowledge records using hybrid retrieval (FAISS + BM25) with neural reranking
  • Generates a grounded, cautious summary with evidence and match relevance
  • Keeps your consultation history private on this device

What it is not

CareLens AI is not a diagnostic device and does not replace a doctor. It provides preliminary health information only. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment. In an emergency, seek immediate professional medical care.

Privacy

All accounts and consultations are stored locally in SQLite on this machine. No symptom data is sent to paid external APIs and there are no analytics trackers.

Technology

FastAPI · SQLite · Sentence Transformers · FAISS · BM25 · Cross-Encoder reranking · local Flan-T5 generation with evidence-based fallback.
