- Base paper named in the batch documents (3.BASE PAPER.docx, 1.C4_BATCH_DETAILS.docx): Y. Nejatbakhsh and M. Aliasgari, "Enhancing Stock Market Prediction With Hybrid Deep Learning: Integrating LSTM, Transformer Attention, Federated Learning, and Sentiment Analysis," IEEE Access, vol. 14, pp. 3926-3942, 2026, doi 10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3649668 (PDF in `Documentary/`). **QuantPulse implements this paper's pipeline.** Supporting references kept in `archived_base_papers/`: X. Zhang, S. Qu, J. Huang, B. Fang, P. S. Yu, "Stock Market Prediction via Multi-Source Multiple Instance Learning," IEEE Access, vol. 6, pp. 50720-50728, 2018, doi 10.1109/ACCESS.2018.2869735 (the paper the project title comes from) and P.-J. Liao et al., "Generalized Stock Price Prediction for Multiple Stocks Combined with News Fusion," arXiv:2603.19286, 2026.
- Batch objectives (6.OBJECTIVES&PROBLEM STATEMENT.docx): (1) collect prices + financial news + social media text; (2) preprocess and align into daily instances per trading day; (3) represent each day as a bag of news instances (Multiple Instance Learning); (4) hybrid model: sequence learning over prices + attention over news content; (5) predict directional movement and evaluate with accuracy, precision, recall, F1; (6) compare multi-source against a price-only baseline.
- **What the product does (and does not do) against those objectives - state plainly everywhere:**
  - Obj 1: prices yes (Yahoo Finance, 10 tickers); financial news only as headlines through NewsAPI **when a key is set**, otherwise a labelled synthetic-headline fallback (this run: synthetic for all ten tickers); **no social-media source**.
  - Obj 2: daily alignment yes, but headlines are collapsed to **one mean sentiment number per trading day** (ffill, 0 before the first headline); not a bag.
  - Obj 3: **Multiple Instance Learning is not implemented in QuantPulse.** Attention in the product runs over the 20-day *price* window (multi-head self-attention after the LSTM), not over news instances.
  - Obj 4: LSTM + 4-head attention + sentiment channel + simulated FedAvg ablation: implemented as in the base paper.
  - Obj 5: the product forecasts **price level** (regression: MAE/MSE/RMSE/R2, plus a directional-accuracy figure and an up/down trend flag). **Precision, recall and F1 are not computed by the product.**
  - Obj 6: ablation LSTM -> +attention -> +sentiment -> hybrid exists, but its sentiment channel is synthetic (see 4.2) so it is not a valid multi-source versus price-only comparison.
  - To address objectives 3, 5 and 6 honestly an **offline MIL study with real headlines was added in this documentation run** (`scripts/mil_study.py`, section 4.5). It is not part of the application.

## 3. Data

- **Prices:** Yahoo Finance daily OHLCV (+ Adj Close) through `yfinance`, 10 tickers: TSLA, AAPL, AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA, META, FDX, IBM, ORCL (the base paper used Tesla, Apple, Airbnb, Amazon, FedEx, IBM, Microsoft, Nvidia, Oracle, Google - Airbnb is replaced by META here; stated in the README). Pull window = last 2 years; the shipped artefacts were trained on **501 rows per ticker ending 2026-09-08** (2024-09-09 to 2026-09-08). Chronological 80/20 split: 400/101 raw rows, 20-day windows give 97 deep-model test windows (8 Jan - 8 Sep 2026 region; see JSON).
- **The application re-downloads prices on every `/api/history` call and overwrites `data/<T>.csv`** (the working tree CSVs end 2026-10-16/20 for some tickers and 2026-09-08/24 for others), so the bundled CSVs are not a stable snapshot. The documentation experiments therefore use a pinned snapshot, `experiments/data_snapshot/<T>.csv` (2024-09-09 to 2026-10-23, 534 rows, `scripts/fetch_snapshot.py`); its rows up to 2026-09-08 reproduce each ticker's `last_close` stored in `artifacts/metrics.json` (checked in `forward_test_shipped`).
- **News in the shipped run:** `news_source = synthetic-fallback` for all ten tickers (no NEWSAPI_KEY): 166-191 template headlines per ticker generated for the **last 90 trading days only** (`sentiment.py`), scored by FinBERT-tone, daily mean, ffill, zero before the first headline. They are produced from 11 hard-coded templates and are independent of the price series. NewsAPI's free tier only returns about a month of history, which is also why the base paper's sentiment experiments span 30 days.
- Base paper's data: Yahoo Finance daily data for ten firms up to 4 Jan 2024; long-term set from IPO, short-term set 6 Jul 2023 - 4 Jan 2024 (127 trading days); hybrid model on a **one-month window** because NewsAPI returned 30 days; split 70/15/15 (hybrid) or 80/20 (baselines).
- **MIL study data (real news):** FNSPID (Dong et al., 'FNSPID: A Comprehensive Financial News Dataset in Time Series', arXiv:2402.06698, 2024) news portion, read from the public mirror `idleengine/financial-news-multisource` (subset `fnspid_news`, shards 027-031; licence 'other' per the mirror card - research use). Headlines of the ten tickers from 2016 onward: 46,532 rows used for 7 tickers; per year {'2016': 717, '2017': 875, '2018': 804, '2019': 603, '2020': 1321, '2021': 1549, '2022': 12107, '2023': 28556}; per ticker {'AAPL': 9100, 'AMZN': 4666, 'FDX': 3406, 'MSFT': 8506, 'NVDA': 8562, 'ORCL': 3880, 'TSLA': 8412}. GOOGL and META have no headlines in this portion and IBM has 7, so they are excluded; **headlines are dense only from 2022**, so the news era is 2022-2023. 100 % of the rows used carry day-level (midnight) stamps, so a headline is only known to the day, not the minute; it is placed in that calendar day's bag. Prices for this study: Yahoo Finance 2016-01-04 to 2023-12-29 (`experiments/data_snapshot/mil/`).

**Findings (all measured):**
- No deep tier beats persistence on real closes (persistence MAE 6.24 vs 29.37 for the best tier on true targets).
- **Evaluation defect:** test targets pass through `apply_scalers`, which clips to [0,1] of the *training* range; for tickers whose test prices rise above the training maximum the target is flat at the clip bound, so MAE is understated and directional accuracy is inflated (e.g. 0.9 for AAPL: flat target and saturated prediction both read 'not up'). 'True' scoring is in the table above.
- **Sentiment channel is empty during training:** synthetic headlines exist only for the last 90 trading days, so the channel is exactly 0 for every training row (0 non-zero rows in the training part for all ten tickers) and non-zero only in the test part. The sentiment tier is trained on a constant channel and tested on random template values, which is why it is worse than the attention tier and unstable across seeds; a placebo whose sentiment values are shuffled in time (so the channel is non-zero in training too) scores the same as the no-sentiment tier. Either way the ablation cannot show a sentiment effect, positive or negative.
- Federated averaging: two rounds of three local epochs over ten clients starting from one global model; the averaged model is evaluated per ticker on its own scalers although the clients use different price scales (MinMax per ticker). It scores worse than the local tiers.

### 4.4 Comparison with the base paper (datasets and protocols differ)

- Base paper (IEEE Access 2026): hybrid model average **R2 0.91** over ten companies (every company above 0.84, several above 0.93), TSLA MSE 0.039 / MAE 0.179 / R2 0.888 (scaled units), baseline LSTM average trend accuracy **65.36 %** over 100 runs per company (Airbnb 94 %, Google 86 %, Tesla 34.9 %, IBM 18 %); Prophet 5-day R2 -0.7126. Test data: roughly the last 15 % of a one-month (30-day) window for the hybrid, a 3-month window for the 5-day LSTM trend accuracy, 5- and 30-day horizons.
- QuantPulse (this project, two years of data, 97 test windows per ticker): shipped R2 mean -67.73 for the hybrid (median -11.21); best deep tier LSTM + attention R2 -15.77; shipped direction metrics 59-68 % but inflated by clipping (true-target direction 53.0 % for LSTM + attention). **The base paper's R2 of 0.91 was not reproduced.** The paper's window (30 days), scaling units and split differ, so the two are not directly comparable. The paper's own limitations section says sentiment was limited to 30 days of NewsAPI headlines and that federated learning was only simulated.

## 7. Defects and caveats observed (state them as limitations; the shipped code was left unchanged)

1. Evaluation clips test targets to the training range (4.2): MAE/R2/direction of the deep tiers are not computed against the real closes.
2. The sentiment channel is zero for every training row and carries synthetic template headlines in the test part (4.2); `README` and the UI disclose the synthetic fallback, but the ablation cannot measure a sentiment effect.
3. Multi-step forecasts roll the Close forward and overwrite Open/High/Low with it (`forecast.py`), keep volume and sentiment constant, and the band uses sqrt(h) growth from one-step residuals; the shipped 5-day forecasts fall 6-28 % for nine of ten tickers (4.1) and missed the realised moves (4.3).
4. FedAvg averages models trained on differently scaled price series (per-ticker MinMax), then scores the global model with each ticker's own scaler.
5. `data_loader.load_ticker` overwrites `data/<T>.csv` on every live pull; `/api/history` therefore performs a network download per request (see latency), and artefacts can be older than the CSVs (the Forecast screen shows a banner explaining this).
6. The synthetic fallback seeds with Python's `hash(ticker)`, which changes between processes (PYTHONHASHSEED); the synthetic headlines are therefore not reproducible unless the hash seed is fixed (the experiments set PYTHONHASHSEED=0).
7. Classical baselines ARIMA/SARIMAX/Prophet forecast the whole 101-day test span without refitting, whereas RF/SVR use actual lagged values one step ahead; the table mixes the two protocols (as in the base paper).
8. No MIL, no social-media text, no precision/recall/F1 in the product (section 1).
